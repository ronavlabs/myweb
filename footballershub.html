Daily Drill:

Passing Drill

How to do it:

  • Place 4 cones in a square about 5 meters apart.
  • Stand at one cone, your partner at the opposite cone.
  • Pass the ball back and forth, focusing on accuracy.
  • After 10 passes, switch positions and repeat.

Shooting Drill

How to do it:

  • Set up the ball 10–15 meters from the goal.
  • Take 10 shots from different angles (left, right, center).
  • Record how many goals you score.
  • Repeat daily and track improvement in the Practice Tracker.

Dribbling Drill

How to do it:

  • Place 6 cones in a straight line, 1 meter apart.
  • Dribble the ball through the cones using both feet.
  • Time yourself and try to improve speed without losing control.
  • Repeat 3–5 times per session.
